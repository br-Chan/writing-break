4612

A "worlds-building" project and short story series about multiverses.

This series contains some of my earliest works and although I've improved since then (and every entry here has been re-edited by 2025-me), chances are these aren't impressive to the common man. Please be nice. :)

:Elevator Pitch

A man drifts alone in a Void, with no memory of how he got there. In his possession is a strange stone tablet with the symbols 4612 engraved on its surface. What does it do? How does he get out of here? What is that squirming dark shape in the distance?

And in his forgotten past is Atro, a boy on his search to find a new home in a medieval fantasy world with a tinge of sci-fi. How does his adventures leave him stranded in the Void?
