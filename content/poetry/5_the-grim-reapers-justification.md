I do not reap souls<br>
in the way you think I do.<br>
I do not take them;<br>
I do not take anything.

The souls yearn for here their own<br>
and I bring them in<br>
and rejuvenate them<br>
and prepare them for another journey<br>
into the world<br>
above.

I sow old souls in these<br>
blackened soils<br>
and reap them anew<br>
to soar again the skies of the living.

In air, as a sparrow--<br>
at sea, as a minnow--<br>
or David Attenborough--<br>
or the life energies of the earth<br>
that turn and turn and turn<br>
round the sun; round each other;<br>
round that collosal wreck;<br>
round you; round me;<br>
round the universe in its monolithic totality.

I do not reap souls<br>
in the way you think I do.<br>
I give them<br>
I give them everything.

*Right?*

~ Brandon Chan
