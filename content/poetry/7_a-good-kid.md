Ten.<br>
Nine. Eight.<br>
Seven. Six.

At lunch, I was told of the ending of this.<br>
Nothing is mended. It remains as it is.<br>
They'd cried in my arms; both of them did. I sit and I miss the urgency of every kiss.

Months now apart, and no one is happy.<br>
The sheets are now cold with my tears and my heart. My chest that had ached as it shook is still shaky.<br>
I wish I were blind, and deaf, and unfeeling. I wish they had never tried to tell me.

I wish this had never happened.<br>
I pray, can I take it -- something is breaking. And I don't think it is something to mend.<br>
Years from now, I shall cry before my children. For I know we will have not heard the end.

She got used to falling asleep in their bed.<br>
Thousands of good mornings, thousands of good nights. So many birthdays no longer to be said.<br>
I mourn at a funeral, of all the little deaths in their hearts. A human together is dead.

I am the anchor that must float for the ship that sinks.<br>
Five. Four.<br>
Three. Two.

One.

~ Brandon Chan
