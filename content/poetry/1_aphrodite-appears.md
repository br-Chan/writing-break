In stories they say<br>
Aphrodite appears<br>
as the one that you are attracted to.<br>

But to me I feel<br>
as though Aphrodite<br>
would appear to me not as a person.<br>

She would be a smile and a laugh.<br>
She would be a song or a film.<br>
She would be a collection of love letters<br>
or the stars under the sky<br>
or the scent of flowers in the air.<br>

She would be the withering flash flood<br>
of all that she says and does and leaves behind.<br>
She would be an impossible, inviolable fingerprint<br>
of her own presence<br>
Unique to all else<br>
in the universe.

~ Brandon Chan
</center>
