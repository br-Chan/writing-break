I walk briskly through the Domain. It is cold, but not deathly so -- cold for Spring, but not for Winter. The breeze flutters through the trees, and I raise an arm to brush off some of the low hanging fronds.

*If True Names were real,* I think to myself, *I will have, for a brief moment, known it.* All of my memories, my aspects of being. All the good.

And all the flaws: scars that fade with time but still simmer through, a noxious aura. With every bump and nudge they grow clearer again, and I slip a little further from the path.

What path? The path I walk upon now, through the Duck Pond? No, surely not. It is the path that I can't see yet, the path that *is* not yet.

The path you only see when you look behind. The path that exists only in hindsight.

I pass by the Wintergarden cafe, and peer through the darkened windows to see the neatly arranged chairs and tables, ready to serve no one for the night. I cross the street and climb up the steps to Wintergarden. But the gates are locked; I'm 2 hours past closing time.

That's okay. My sister suggested I come here when I asked her where I could walk. I can walk back, and will be just as fulfilled. I jump down from the ledge and trot back round to the pond. I take a seat at a hexagonal bench, adorned with plaques of lore. I take out my phone to type.

Cold and beautiful is the Path in Hindsight.

I always- well, not always. I've dreamed of being with someone I love, and sitting with them in the shade when the shade is everywhere. And to be content.

*Adapted from a piece of writing from October 28, 2023. I had taken a walk while I was living at Grafton Hall, my 1st year university hall. 2 weeks after I wrote this, I left Grafton Hall -- never to return.*
